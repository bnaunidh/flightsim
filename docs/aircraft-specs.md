# The roster against the real aeroplanes

Generated by `node tools/aircraft-specs.mjs` from `src/aircraft/types.js` (and `extra/`) and the published figures in `tools/real-aircraft-specs.mjs`. `tests/features/aircraft-specs.mjs` holds every type to the bands below and fails if this file is stale — do not edit it by hand.

## What the game scales on purpose

- **Mass.** The undercarriage model caps every leg at 160 kN, so nothing weighs more than about 28 t: the big aeroplanes fly as lightly loaded versions of themselves, with the real span, length and inertia per kilogram, and the ratios of thrust and wing to mass kept.
- **Thrust-to-weight.** The airliners carry about 0.36 (a real one has 0.22–0.32 at MTOW and 0.45–0.6 empty) so they unstick inside the 1,100 m Kestrel strip; the giants (BelugaXL, Dreamlifter, An-225, C-5) carry 0.26 and want a 2,000 m runway, which is the point of them; the C-17 carries 0.40 because the real one is built for short strips. Every game figure sits between the real aeroplane's ratio at MTOW and its ratio empty.
- **Stalling speeds.** Wing loadings of 180–250 kg/m² against the real 600–700 put the stall at 65–85 kt instead of 105–130, so a child can land them. The fighters are lighter than the real jets with more lift at low speed, for the same runway; what is kept honest is the relative feel (the Raptor has the best thrust-to-weight and is the fastest of the real jets, the Hornet the gentlest approach, the A-10 the slowest).
- **Ceilings** were not scaled — they did not exist. The exponential atmosphere alone let the trainer climb to 26,000 ft and an A320 to 52,000. Every type now carries its published service ceiling, and above 1.05× it the air the flight model uses thins faster (to 0.35 of itself at 1.25×, `physics.js ceilingFactor`): less thrust, less lift, the stall nearer. The top of the sky stays at 70,000 ft.
- **Indicated limits (Vne).** The airliners' are their published Vmo (the A380, A350, 777 and Q400 were 6–9 % high and were brought to it); the fighters' are 65–85 % of the real jets' — a child-safe red line, not a performance claim; the A-10's 330 kt is the slowest jet on purpose.

## Speeds, climb, ceiling

Game speeds are worked out from the aerodynamics (CLmax = CL0 + CLa·αstall, full flap adds 0.62); Vr is taken as 1.1 × Vs0 and Vref as 1.3 × Vs0, the way the hangar quotes them. The game climb is the best excess power at sea level, clean; the game's absolute ceiling is where full thrust meets minimum drag. Real figures marked ≈ are estimates.

## Notes per type

- **skylark** — the 172 to the number: the same wing, the same weight, a 40 kt stall, 159 kt on the red line; its climb is 1.7× a real 172's because the flight model's propeller does not lose power with height the way a normally aspirated engine does, which is what the 14,000 ft ceiling now stands in for.
- **courier** — a Bonanza-class tourer; honest mass, wing and power, a slightly softer stall.
- **meridian** — a 40-seat regional jet of the game's own with a bigger wing than an ERJ (78 m² for 16.5 t); thrust-to-weight 0.37, inside a lightly loaded ERJ-145's 0.31–0.56.
- **vanguard, osprey** — the game's own fighter and carrier jet, in the Mirage 2000's and the A-7's bands; both lighter and slower-stalling for the Kestrel strip (see types.js).
- **tempest** — a twin where the hurricane hunter it is built on is a four-engined P-3; the wing and ceiling are the Orion's class, the red line 340 kt against its 405.
- **nightjar** — an original flying wing in the B-2's bands; thrust-to-weight 0.42 against the B-2's 0.18 (MTOW) to 0.44 (empty).
- **harrier (Skyhook)** — a light helicopter of the H125's class; the rotor, not a wing, so no stall or thrust-to-weight is quoted; its ceiling is the H125's 23,000 ft.
- **f22, f35b, fa18, a10** — real jets at a third of their mass with the real relative feel; thrust-to-weight 0.63–0.99 inside each one's MTOW→empty band; red lines 65–85 % of the real ones on purpose.
- **massimo, tpose** — the game's own record racer and the man who flies on its numbers: no real counterpart, so only the in-game ordering is held (fastest of all, highest red line, a ceiling above every real type's).
- **airliners (a320 … b777, concorde)** — the real span and wing proportions at a fifth to a fifteenth of the real mass; thrust-to-weight 0.36 inside every one's MTOW→empty band; Vmo matched.
- **giants (belugaxl, dreamlifter, an225, c5)** — thrust-to-weight 0.26 (at or a little under the real MTOW figures: deliberate — they need Gateway's 3,800 m), stall near 85 kt because the game grades a touchdown over 120 kt as a crash.
- **b1b, c17** — 0.40 against real 0.26 → 0.64 and 0.28 → 0.57: lightly loaded, inside the band; the B-1B's swing wing changes CLa, CL0 and the stall with sweep (swing-wing.js) and the table shows it spread.
